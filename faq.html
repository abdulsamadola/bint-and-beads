Sizing & Measurement Guide

Wrap a flexible tape measure or paper strip around your wrist bone without slack. Note the centimeter measurement: XS (15cm), S (16.5cm), M (17.5cm), L (19cm), XL (20.5cm). If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort.

Our waist beads arrive as traditional tie-on strands with extra organic cotton cord length (Standard fits up to 45 inches, Extended fits up to 55 inches). You simply wrap the strand around your preferred waistline, push the beads snugly into place, knot three times securely, and snip away the excess thread.

Bead Care & Longevity

Our traditional glass waist beads are strung on reinforced cotton made specifically for permanent water exposure. For bracelets, baroque pearls, and metallic chokers, we recommend removing them before swimming or showering to preserve the mirror lustre of the gold accents.

Always store each piece separated in its complimentary Bint velvet dust pouch to avoid friction between crystals and harder metals.
