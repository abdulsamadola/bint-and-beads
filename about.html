ORIGINS

OUR STORY

Bint & Beads was founded on a simple conviction: beadwork is one of Africa’s highest artistic disciplines.

Across generations, beads have marked ceremonies of passage, embodied royalty, celebrated feminine curves, and functioned as sacred heirlooms. Yet modern fashion had frequently relegated beadwork to fast-fashion novelties or uniform factory assembly.

We established our studio in Lagos to restore the tactile dignity of the craft. Every design honors traditional stringing traditions while embracing contemporary architectural silhouettes, rich noir palettes, and precious metallic finishes.

THE ATELIER PROCESS

CRAFTED BY HAND

No machines. No assembly lines. Only patient hands, good daylight, and uncompromising focus.

Each piece requires hours of meticulous hand-selection. Beads are examined individually under natural studio daylight to ensure consistency in cut, light reflection, and weight. Faceted Austrian glass crystals are paired with natural freshwater pearls and durable 18k PVD gold-plated spacers resistant to tropical humidity.

"When you wear an accessory made slowly by hand, you carry an artisan’s concentrated focus and genuine care wherever you go."
