STEP 06

Select your fit

Ensuring your bespoke piece rests comfortably and securely.

For Wrist: Wrap a soft measuring tape or a string around your wrist bone snugly without slack. Mark where it meets, then measure against a flat ruler. If between measurements, we recommend sizing up by 0.5cm for comfort.

For Waist Beads: Decide whether you prefer your beads high at your natural waist, below your belly button, or relaxed on your hips. Measure directly against bare skin.
